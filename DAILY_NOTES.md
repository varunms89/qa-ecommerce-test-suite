### 📅 2026-11-13 (Friday) — Playwright Auto-Waiting & Resilient Locators
- **Core Concept:** Playwright performs actionability checks (visible, stable, enabled, attached) before clicking or filling. Prefer user-facing locators like page.get_by_role() and data-test attributes over fragile brittle XPath selectors.

- **Status:** Verified in production & automated test pipelines.

### 📅 2026-11-14 (Saturday) — Shift-Left Testing in Agile SDLC
- **Core Concept:** Testing begins during the requirement specification and design phase rather than waiting for code completion. Catching defects during requirement review is 10x cheaper than fixing bugs in system integration testing.
- **Author:** VARUN M S
- **Status:** Verified in production & automated test pipelines.

### 📅 2026-11-15 (Sunday) — Defect Life Cycle & Disputed Bug Resolution
- **Core Concept:** When a developer marks a bug as 'Not a Bug', reproduce it systematically, capture DevTools console/network logs, and verify against the SRS/BRD. If ambiguity persists, present the reproducible steps to the Product Owner/QA Lead.
- **Author:** VARUN M S
- **Status:** Verified in production & automated test pipelines.

### 📅 2026-11-16 (Monday) — Boundary Value Analysis (BVA) 6-Point Strategy
- **Core Concept:** For any numeric boundary [Min, Max], the 6 critical test points are Min-1 (Invalid), Min (Valid), Min+1 (Valid), Max-1 (Valid), Max (Valid), and Max+1 (Invalid). Statistical studies show over 60% of logic bugs occur at boundary extremes.
- **Author:** VARUN M S
- **Status:** Verified in production & automated test pipelines.

---

### 📅 2026-11-17 (Tuesday) — Equivalence Class Partitioning (ECP)
- **Core Concept:** Divide test inputs into mutually exclusive equivalence partitions where all members are assumed to be processed identically by the system. Test one typical representative per partition to optimize test efficiency without reducing defect detection.
- **Author:** VARUN M S
- **Status:** Verified in production & automated test pipelines.
